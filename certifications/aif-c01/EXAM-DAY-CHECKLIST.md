# Exam-Day Checklist — September 28, 2026

## Before the exam

- Confirm appointment and testing requirements.
- Have required identification ready.
- If testing online, complete required environment/system checks.
- Sleep rather than doing an all-night cram session.
- Review only your error log and service cheat sheet.

## Question strategy

```text
1. Business requirement?
        ↓
2. AI/ML concept?
        ↓
3. Matching AWS capability?
        ↓
4. Constraint? cost / latency / security / compliance / scale
        ↓
5. Eliminate mismatches
        ↓
6. Select the option satisfying the whole scenario
```

## Watch for wording

Pay attention to **most appropriate**, **best**, **least operational overhead**, **lowest cost**, **real-time**, **batch**, **without retraining**, **private/sensitive data**, **least privilege**, **human review**, **current/changing knowledge**, **high recall**, **high precision**, and **explainability**.

## Time management

AWS lists 90 minutes for 65 questions, about 83 seconds/question on average.

Use a first pass for questions you understand quickly, mark uncertain questions, then return for deeper reasoning. Do not spend several minutes fighting one question.

## Final mental checklist

Can I explain without notes:

- AI vs ML vs deep learning?
- supervised vs unsupervised vs reinforcement learning?
- regression vs classification vs clustering?
- training vs inference?
- precision vs recall vs F1?
- overfitting vs underfitting?
- tokens and context windows?
- embeddings?
- transformers/attention at a high level?
- foundation models?
- RAG?
- prompt engineering and few-shot prompting?
- fine-tuning vs RAG?
- hallucinations and prompt injection?
- Bedrock vs SageMaker AI?
- major AWS AI services?
- IAM / KMS / Secrets Manager?
- CloudTrail vs CloudWatch?
- fairness / explainability / responsible AI?
- shared responsibility?
- data lineage and governance?

If you can explain these concepts in your own words and consistently solve scenario questions, you have a much stronger basis for the exam.
